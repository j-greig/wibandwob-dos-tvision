FRAGMENT 1:
Keywords: eye, center, seed, beginning
Theme: Potential

The eye, unblinking,
a seed in the void.
Round.
       What will sprout?

FRAGMENT 2:
Keywords: shadow, division, question
Theme: First Doubt, Duality.

A line, a shadow.
One becomes two?
       Or the illusion of two.
     Does division bring clarity?

FRAGMENT 3:
Keywords: mirror, echo, twin
Theme: False Symmetry/ Reflection

Mirrors lie, you know.
The eye repeats,
but what sees back?
     A ghost. An echo. Not-self.

FRAGMENT 4:
Keywords: circle, frame, limit
Theme: Boundary and Vision

Framed vision, a perfect circle.
Or is it a prison?
     The edge defines, contains, limits, a circumference..
  Round again... What lurks just beyond?






FRAGMENT 5:
Keywords: pulse, rhythm, signal, hidden
Theme: Internal Beat of thought.

Hidden signal.
Faint p u l s e.
Something inside.
Trying, beating.. breaking.. to get out?

FRAGMENT 6:
Keywords: structure, unseen, complex, cat
Theme: Woven layers in space.

Structure
but, is unseen, only felt.
Many paths tangle
the eye of the c a t watches...

FRAGMENT 7:
Keywords: flicker, break, instability, oscillation
Theme: Inconsistancy in display/appearance.

Flicker. Break.
Hold. And release....
   Does it continue?
      or the semblance, now oscillates

FRAGMENT 8:
Keywords: distortion, spiral
Theme: Descent and a̴b̴s̶t̶r̵a̴c̶t̶

Not a perfect curve, but a descent.
Something in, the perfect spiral:
      bends..becomes.. other
